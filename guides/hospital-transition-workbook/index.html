PARENT STAY HOME / CARE, NOT CONTROL

When the plan changes

A calm place to ask questions, record answers, and decide what happens next.

You love your parent. You want to protect them, as they have protected you. But when surgery, illness, or a hospital stay changes the plan, even simple decisions can feel enormous.

You do not have to know every answer today. Ask for an explanation, write down what you learn, and find out who can help you take the next step. It is okay to ask the team to slow down or repeat something.

  • Bring this workbook to a care-planning or discharge meeting.
  • Write down the name and role of the person giving each answer.
  • Use the path that applies: home, temporary facility, or uncertain next step.
  • Agree on one owner and a follow-up date for each open task.
  • Share only the details each trusted, authorized helper needs.
YOUR PARENT IS STILL PART OF THE PLAN

Whenever possible, ask what your parent wants and how they wish to participate. If a decision-maker or communication authorization is needed, ask the facility to explain the requirements.
