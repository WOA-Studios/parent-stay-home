Can My Parent Safely Stay Home?

A calm starting point for thinking through safety, support, and next steps.

Start with these steps

  • Look at immediate safety concerns
  • Notice whether food, medication, hygiene, and bills are being managed
  • Consider whether help can reduce risk
  • Talk with your parent respectfully
  • Ask professionals for guidance when safety is unclear

You may not need to solve everything today.

Choose the item that feels most urgent and take one practical step. If there is immediate danger, call emergency services or the appropriate local crisis resource.
