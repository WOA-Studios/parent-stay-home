What to Do When Your Parent Can't Drive

Find practical transportation options and reduce isolation.

Start with these steps

  • List essential destinations
  • Check county and senior transportation
  • Ask about medical rides
  • Look for grocery and pharmacy delivery
  • Build a backup ride plan

You may not need to solve everything today.

Choose the item that feels most urgent and take one practical step. If there is immediate danger, call emergency services or the appropriate local crisis resource.
