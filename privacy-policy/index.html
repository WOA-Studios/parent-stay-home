Privacy

Privacy Policy

Parent Stay Home respects visitor privacy and keeps this site focused on practical information, resources, and next steps.

Information you choose to send

Parent Stay Home may collect information you choose to send by email or through a submission form, such as your name, email address, organization name, website, phone number, service area, and message.

How information may be used

Information submitted to Parent Stay Home may be used to review suggested resources, respond to questions, correct listings, improve the site, or follow up about a provider submission.

Analytics and site improvement

Parent Stay Home may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site, which pages are helpful, and where improvements may be needed.

Personal information

Parent Stay Home does not sell personal information. Please do not send private medical, financial, legal, or sensitive personal information through this site.

Future updates

This policy may be updated if new features are added, including email signup, advertising, downloadable tools, hosted forms, or analytics services.
