Keep the Plan Ready

Care made ready also means care kept ready.

This V1 page is for reviewing the plan and replacing outdated paper pages. It does not store your parent’s personal information online.

Choose a review cadence

  • Every 3 months
  • Every 6 months
  • Every 12 months
  • After any event that shows the plan needs to change

Reconfirm these people and details

  • Does each responder still want and have capacity for the role?
  • Has anyone moved, become ill, changed phone numbers, or lost home access?
  • Is the caregiver/helper schedule still correct?
  • Do authorized access arrangements still work?
  • Are doctor, pharmacy, insurance, hospital, and health-care-agent details current?
  • Are the parent-local non-emergency and 10-digit law-enforcement numbers still current?
  • Does the parent still agree with the plan?
