Can Someone Get Inside?

A responder who reaches the house still needs a safe, authorized way to help.

Decide this with your parent while things are calm

  • Who is authorized to enter the home?
  • Who has a key, if anyone?
  • Can a building manager, front desk, landlord, or trusted neighbor assist?
  • If a lockbox or smart lock is used, who is authorized to receive access instructions?
  • Are there pets, alarms, stairs, gates, or other access issues?
  • Where is the Mom Didn’t Answer binder kept?
