Please Check on My Parent

Simple instructions for the trusted person who has agreed to go to the home.

If you believe there is an immediate emergency:Contact the emergency system appropriate to the parent’s location and follow the call-taker’s instructions.

When you arrive

  • Confirm the correct address.
  • See whether the parent responds.
  • Use only access you are authorized to use.
  • Be aware of pets, alarms, stairs, or other access concerns.
  • If responders are coming, help provide legitimate access if you can.
  • Call the adult child or primary contact once you know what is happening.
DO NOT ask an untrained helper to diagnose symptoms, provide medical treatment, or move a person who has fallen unless appropriately trained or instructed by emergency professionals.

Contacts to have before you go

Adult child / primary contact
Alternate contact
Home-access contact
Parent’s address

For privacy, send sensitive access details separately and only to people who need them for their agreed role.
