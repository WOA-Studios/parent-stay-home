About Parent Stay Home

Parent Stay Home was created to help adult children and caregivers find practical information, services, resources, and next steps when an aging parent starts needing more support at home.

Why this exists

Families should not have to go from site to site, office to office, and voicemail to voicemail while already overwhelmed. This site is designed to make one part of a difficult season easier.

What we do

We organize practical guides, checklists, and researched resource listings so families have a calmer place to start.

What we do not do

We do not provide medical, legal, financial, or care-management advice. We do not endorse providers. We help you identify options and questions so you can make informed next steps.

Parent Stay Home

Practical resources, checklists, and next steps for adult children and caregivers helping an aging parent remain safely at home for as long as reasonably possible.

Listings are informational and are not endorsements. This site provides general information only and is not medical, legal, financial, or care-management advice.
