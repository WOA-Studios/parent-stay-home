Find help with grab bars, ramps, bathroom safety, accessibility updates, and home modifications that may make daily life safer.

Before you call

Use these questions to reduce back-and-forth and help you get to the right person faster.

  • Ask whether they provide assessments, installation, or both.
  • Confirm licensing, insurance, and accessibility experience.
  • Start with bathrooms, stairs, entryways, lighting, and emergency access.
Reminder: Contact each organization directly to confirm current services, service area, eligibility, cost, and availability.
