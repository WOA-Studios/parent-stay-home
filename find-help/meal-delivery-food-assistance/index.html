Meal Delivery & Food Assistance

Find home-delivered meals, food assistance, senior nutrition programs, and related food support.

Before you call

Use these questions to reduce back-and-forth and help you get to the right person faster.

  • Ask about eligibility, delivery schedule, and meal type.
  • Confirm whether special diets can be accommodated.
  • Ask whether the program is temporary, ongoing, free, low-cost, or private pay.
Reminder: Contact each organization directly to confirm current services, service area, eligibility, cost, and availability.
