Important Papers Checklist for Aging Parents

Know where the most important documents are before you urgently need them.

What to gather

  • Will or estate documents
  • Power of attorney
  • Advance directive or healthcare proxy
  • Insurance policies
  • Bank and benefit information
  • Tax records
  • Property documents
  • Funeral or final arrangement information

How to use this

Start with what you already know. Leave blanks where needed. The goal is not perfection; the goal is to make the next emergency or appointment less chaotic.
