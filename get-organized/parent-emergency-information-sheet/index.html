Start with what you know. Leave blanks where needed. The goal is not perfection. The goal is to make the next call, appointment, or emergency less chaotic.

  • Keep a copy somewhere easy to find.
  • Share only with people your parent trusts or has authorized, when appropriate.
  • Review it after medication changes, new doctors, moves, or major health changes.
