Long-Distance Caregiving Checklist

Coordinate support when you do not live nearby.

What to gather

  • Local emergency contact
  • Trusted neighbor
  • House key access
  • Ride options
  • Meal support
  • Medication information
  • Doctor contacts
  • Backup caregiver plan

How to use this

Start with what you already know. Leave blanks where needed. The goal is not perfection; the goal is to make the next emergency or appointment less chaotic.

Printable downloads can be added after the first site shell is live.
