Get Organized

Get organized before there is a crisis.

When a parent starts needing more help, important information can be hard to find quickly. These tools can help you gather what matters before you urgently need it.

You do not have to organize everything today.

Start with the tool that matches what feels most urgent. Each tool is designed for personal or family use, with print and download options so you can keep your own copy.

  • Use what helps right now.
  • Leave blanks where you are not sure.
  • Update the information as things change.
Privacy note: These tools are for your personal or family use. Parent Stay Home does not receive, save, store, or see anything you type into them. Use the print or download options to keep your own copy. Share completed copies only with people your parent trusts or has authorized, when appropriate.

A simple order

If everything feels urgent, start here.

You do not have to complete every tool today. Start with the information that would matter most if something happened this week.

  1. Parent Emergency Information Sheet — what someone may need fast.
  2. Important Papers Checklist — where key documents are kept.
  3. Home Safety Checklist — what may need attention in the home.
  4. Long-Distance Caregiving Checklist — who can help when you are not nearby.
  5. Financial Information Checklist — what bills, accounts, insurance, and benefits may need attention.
General information note: These tools are for organization and planning only. They are not medical, legal, financial, or care-management advice. For decisions about healthcare, legal documents, benefits, taxes, accounts, medications, or safety concerns, contact the appropriate qualified professional, agency, or emergency service.
