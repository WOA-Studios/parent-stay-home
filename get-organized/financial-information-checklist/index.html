Get Organized

Financial Information Checklist for Aging Parents

Organize financial basics so bills, benefits, insurance, and important contacts are easier to find during care planning or an unexpected change.

Download blank PDF

Use this to know what exists and where it is kept.

Financial information can be scattered across mail, email, online accounts, paper files, wallets, drawers, and memory. This checklist helps families gather basic information before an emergency makes everything harder.

  • Start with bills, income/benefits, insurance, accounts, taxes, and trusted professionals.
  • Leave blanks where needed. The goal is awareness and organization, not perfection.
  • Share only with people your parent trusts or has legally authorized, when appropriate.
Privacy note: This checklist is for your personal/family use. Parent Stay Home does not receive, save, store, or see anything you type here. Use the download or print button to keep your own copy.
