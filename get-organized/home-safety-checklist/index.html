Get Organized

Home Safety Checklist for Aging Parents

Look room by room for common safety concerns at home before a fall, injury, or emergency makes the next step harder.

Download blank PDF

Start with what you can see.

You do not have to inspect everything perfectly. Walk through the home slowly and write down the concerns that feel most urgent.

  • Focus first on fall risks, bathroom safety, stairs, lighting, emergency access, and medication areas.
  • Take photos of concerns if that helps you explain them to family or a professional.
  • Use the action tracker to decide what needs attention now, next, or later.
Privacy note: This checklist is for your personal/family use. Parent Stay Home does not receive, save, store, or see anything you type here. Use the download or print button to keep your own copy.

Home and parent information

Quick safety scan

Check anything that may need a closer look. This is not a professional inspection. It is a starting point for noticing concerns and deciding what to ask about next.

Safety note: If there is an immediate danger, such as a recent fall, fire risk, gas smell, unsafe electrical issue, wandering risk, or urgent medical concern, contact emergency services, the appropriate utility, or a qualified professional right away.
